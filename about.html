I'm a 2D animator, storyboard artist and compositor. I make characters and worlds that people remember, from cinematic 2D trailers to short pieces for TikTok and YouTube.

I've worked as an in-betweener and compositor at Nsibidi Fables, where I contributed to Amanirenas: The Kush Empire, as art director and lead storyboard artist at Penmob Creative Studios on The New Snack City Show, and as an animator on comedy shorts for Franken Studio.

I also run D'Paper Man, my own studio, where I'm making Burdens in Babylon, a Bible series in anime style.

Outside animation I build automation systems with n8n, GoHighLevel and AI models, for creative teams and for other kinds of business.

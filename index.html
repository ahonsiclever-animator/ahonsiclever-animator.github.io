Process

How a project runs

  1. 01 · Brief

    You send the idea

    The script or idea, the format you need (9:16, 16:9 or 1:1), references and the deadline.

  2. 02 · Boards

    We agree the shots

    I board it and cut a rough animatic so timing and camera are settled before any final art.

  3. 03 · Animation

    I animate

    Character animation in Moho or Clip Studio Paint, then clean-up and colour.

  4. 04 · Delivery

    You get finished files

    Lighting and effects in After Effects, then exports for every platform you post on.
